The user (owner of the Bally The Shadow) says they are not very experienced and fear overheating a trace or lifting pads when repairing the Power Driver board (stated 2026-10-25).

When the bench repair comes, give conservative, step-by-step soldering guidance: cut the old transistor legs close to the body and remove each leg separately, add flux and fresh solder before desoldering, keep heat short (3–5 s per pad), use a temperature-controlled iron with a large enough tip, practise on scrap boards first. Mention that a lifted pad is repairable and that the board work can be delegated to a repairer, since the machine-side checks need no soldering. See [[shadow-diagnostic-method]].
